09C has no official price. Any price is only the price agreed by a buyer and seller in a completed trade.

How early trading works

Bitcoin 09 is still in the early mining phase. There is no official exchange market. The first market is community OTC: one person wants to buy, one person wants to sell, and they agree on the terms themselves.

Where to post

Use the #💱-otc-trading channel in Discord for community buy/sell posts.

Ground rules

  • Trade small first.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, remote access, or wallet files.
  • Staff will not DM you first and does not provide official escrow.
  • No fake volume, wash trades, pump groups, price promises, or impersonation.
  • Keep completed-trade references factual so the community can see real price discovery.
