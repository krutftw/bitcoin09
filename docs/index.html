Bitcoin 09 (09C)

the coin that you can mine like it's 2009

Network status: live explorer. Current release: v0.1.9.

Bitcoin 09 is Bitcoin, same idea top to bottom, changed in one place: the proof of work is Argon2id (memory-hard) instead of SHA-256. Normal CPUs do the mining and ASICs/GPUs get no real advantage.

Mine it

Download the latest release for your OS, then run:

btc09 node -mine

That's it. The seed node is built in. Your wallet is created automatically and your reward address prints at startup. When your computer finds a block it prints BLOCK FOUND and you own 50 09C.

09C is worth nothing. Bitcoin was worth nothing in 2009 too, that's the whole idea. No premine, no allocation, no promises. The genesis reward is burned, so every coin that will ever exist gets mined by someone's CPU. Mine it because it's fun and you missed 2009.
